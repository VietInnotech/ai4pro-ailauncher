# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Technical operators, support engineers, and end users who need a single local desktop app to start, stop, restart, and monitor `Local AI` on a machine that has already been prepared for them. End users should only see safe status and the few actions they need. Operators need predictable behavior, low-friction troubleshooting, and a clear boundary between simple control and deeper diagnostics.

## Product Purpose

Provide a cross-platform desktop launcher for locally hosted AI services, with one aggregate user-facing service called `Local AI` that manages the underlying language and speech inference engines. The product exists to make local AI feel operationally simple on prepared machines, while keeping setup, engine details, model placement, and diagnostics out of the normal user flow.

Success means a user can tell whether `Local AI` is ready, running, or needs attention, and can safely take the right action without seeing internal complexity.

## Brand Personality

Plain, simple, and boring in the best possible way. Government-style clarity, no hype, no novelty for its own sake, no consumer-AI theatrics. The voice should be calm, precise, and matter-of-fact. The interface should feel dependable, administrative, and intentionally unflashy.

## Anti-references

Avoid anything that feels like a startup dashboard, AI demo app, crypto terminal, gamer UI, or glossy consumer product. Do not use flashy gradients, neon accents, playful mascots, dense analytics panels, or language that implies magic, intelligence theater, or marketplace-style discovery. Avoid over-designed empty states, overly clever copy, and UI that makes simple service control feel experimental.

## Design Principles

- Keep the primary flow to a small number of obvious actions.
- Hide internal machinery unless the user intentionally enters Developer Mode.
- Use safe, generic status language in Simple Mode.
- Prefer predictability over novelty, especially in controls and error handling.
- Make the difference between normal operation and diagnostics explicit in behavior, not just appearance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA where practical. Support keyboard navigation, strong focus states, readable type, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions. Status should be understandable without relying on color alone. Error and attention states should remain clear with plain language and stable layout.
